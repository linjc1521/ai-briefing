Lead story · Anthropic · Maths

An Anthropic mathematician says he proved the Riemann Hypothesis with Claude

A mathematician at Anthropic, Levent Alpöge, says he found a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis working with an unreleased Claude model. The problem has been open for 167 years. The proof is written in Lean, a language that lets software check every step.

The Lean and Mathlib communities now say they "certify that both the statement and the proof … are correct," and MIT's Andrew Sutherland compiled it separately.

Number theorist Andrew Granville called the write-up "garbled" and questioned the timing so soon after the IPO. On the same day, a separate, fully human proof by Alessandro Masullo was also posted.
