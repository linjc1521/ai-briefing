Lead story · Anthropic & OpenAI · Maths

Claude and an OpenAI model post VP ≠ VNP proofs five hours apart

VP versus VNP is the algebraic cousin of P versus NP, which Leslie Valiant posed in 1979. It uses arithmetic circuits instead of binary logic. On 23 Nov (US time), Anthropic put a 70-page paper, "VP ≠ VNP over every field", on GitHub with the author line "Claude, Anthropic", along with a Lean formalisation. The README says an internal research Claude model discovered the result and that "no human mathematician has checked the proof."

About five hours later, OpenAI posted a 30-page manuscript, "VP and VNP are different", from "an unnamed internal OpenAI model". Its notes date the result to 6 Nov and leave credit and precedence to mathematicians. An independent analysis by sysnote built Anthropic's Lean proof cleanly and found it covers the paper's main bound. OpenAI's Lean proves only the plain (exact-circuit) separation, not its stronger headline bound. Both papers say VP ≠ VNP does not imply P ≠ NP.
