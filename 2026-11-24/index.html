Claude gets a zero-data-retention option for its top models

Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) is now in public beta. In June, Anthropic said it would keep prompts and responses on its most capable models (Mythos and Fable) for 30 days to catch misuse. With EFS, that data is encrypted and stored in the customer's own S3 or Google Cloud bucket, and Anthropic keeps no copy.

Automated monitoring scans the data and flags "Safety Cases" for the customer's own team to review. Anthropic doesn't charge for EFS, and it works on the Claude Platform, AWS, Bedrock and Google Cloud. Anthropic says it built EFS with more than 100 customers, including banks and law firms.

ZhipuModels

Zhipu's GLM-5.4 runs its layers twice

Zhipu (Z.ai) released GLM-5.4 on 23 Nov. It's its first flagship built on a "Loop Transformer". It runs 28 layers of its 750B GLM-5 base model twice, so the model reasons more deeply without adding parameters. Zhipu says it reaches the level of 2 to 3 trillion parameter models, but hasn't published official benchmark scores yet.

It's live on the GLM Coding Plan, ZCode and Z.ai Chat. The API is "coming soon", and Zhipu will release open weights after safety hardening. The Paper reports that a roughly 3T-parameter GLM-5.5 is widely expected in December.

South Australia trials a screen-free day each week in primary schools

From 2027, ten SA public primary schools will switch devices off one day a week for Years 3 to 6, in a six-month trial. Reception to Year 2 will have no screens at all. The pen licence is coming back, and department devices will replace students' own laptops in the trial. "We don't want a situation where technology has been doing the schooling for kids," Premier Malinauskas said.
